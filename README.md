# 🏥 UPSC Combined Medical Services (CMS) Question Papers (2011–2025)

A comprehensive, year-wise archive of **official UPSC Combined Medical Services (CMS) Examination** question papers. 

### 🎯 About the Exam
The **UPSC CMS Examination** is a prestigious national-level recruitment exam conducted by the Union Public Service Commission. It is specifically designed for **MBBS graduates** to secure **Group 'A' (Gazetted) Medical Officer roles** in various Central Government organizations (Indian Railways, Central Health Services, NDMC/MCD, Ordnance Factories, etc.).

> 📌 *Covers: 15 Years (2011–2025) | Target Audience: MBBS Graduates | Format: Official MCQ Papers*

---

## 📊 UPSC CMS Exam Pattern (Computer Based Examination)

To help aspirants practice effectively, this repository follows the official UPSC CMS CBE structure. Each year contains two objective-type papers:

| Paper | Subjects Covered | No. of Questions | Max Marks | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Paper I** | General Medicine (96) <br> Paediatrics (24) | 120 | 250 | 2 Hours |
| **Paper II** | Surgery (40) <br> Gynaecology & Obstetrics (40) <br> Preventive & Social Medicine (40) | 120 | 250 | 2 Hours |

*(Note: Qualifying the written CBE leads to the Personality Test / Interview worth 100 Marks).*
