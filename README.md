# 🏥 UPSC Combined Medical Services (CMS) Question Papers (2011–2025)

A complete, year-wise archive of **official UPSC CMS exam papers** covering 15 years of clinical MCQ examinations. Includes Paper I & Paper II focusing on **General Medicine, Pediatrics, Surgery, Gynaecology & Obstetrics, and Preventive & Social Medicine (PSM)**.
